Works with the theme you already have

    Varn is a theme app extension, so it works on any Online Store 2.0 theme. Instead of building its own picker, it drives the one your theme already ships, and steps aside entirely when your theme has native swatches for that option.

    Setup

    Five steps, and the app does four of them.

    The setup wizard checks your theme for you, finds your color options for you, and names your colors for you. You confirm, and you are live.

    1. 1 Turn it on

      One button opens your theme editor with the Varn app embed already selected. Toggle it on, hit save, done. Varn then verifies it is live by reading your theme’s own settings, so the checkmark is a fact, not a guess.

      Once per theme

    2. 2 Pick a product

      Search your catalog, filter to products that actually have a color option, and pick one. Or skip ahead and hit Auto-configure all products to do the whole catalog in one pass.

      Free on every plan

    3. 3 Confirm the options

      Only color options are offered, so Size and Material are never turned into swatches by accident. Varn recognizes Color, Colour, Couleur, Farbe, Colore, Kleur and Farve out of the box, and you can teach it your own name.

    4. 4 Let AI setup fill in the colors

      One click reads your option names and fills in the colors it is confident about, in ten languages. When a name is not a color, a vision model looks at the product photo instead. Everything stays editable, and a live preview shows the result as you work.

      On every plan, including Free

    5. 5 Go live

      Save, and the swatches are on your storefront. Nothing was written into your theme, and nothing is left behind if you ever uninstall.

    Product groups

    Separate products, one swatch row.

    Plenty of stores list each color as its own product: Aria Tee, Clay; Aria Tee, Blush; Aria Tee, Lilac. Shoppers land on one and never find out the others exist. A group joins those products into one color row on every member’s page.

    • Name the row shoppers see, for example “Also in”. That caption sits above the chips on each member’s product page.
    • Add products, then drag. Search, tick, add. The member order is the swatch order shoppers see.
    • A product lives in one group. If it already belongs elsewhere, Varn shows a conflict and a Move here action, so nothing sits in two groups by mistake.
    • Find groups for me. Varn clusters titles that differ only by a color-like suffix, then you add, edit or dismiss each suggestion.
    • If colors are variants of one product, skip this. Groups are for the separate-products case. Ordinary swatches cover variants.

    How Varn behaves in your store

    Exactly what it touches, and what it leaves alone.

    Four design decisions that shape how Varn runs on your storefront.

    It loads deferred, and only what you use

    The storefront script is deferred and split by feature, so a page downloads only the part your settings actually need, and the build enforces a size budget on it. Swatch data is read by your theme at render time, so Varn adds no request for it and no layout shift.

    deferred, split by feature

    Your theme still owns the cart

    Varn does not build its own variant picker. Clicking a swatch selects the option in your theme’s own control, so add to cart is still pure Shopify and the wrong variant cannot reach a checkout. If our script never loads, the shopper still gets your theme’s working dropdown.

    progressive enhancement, by design

    Uninstalling removes it completely

    Varn is a theme app extension, not a script tag, so there is nothing to inject and nothing to clean up. Uninstall it and your theme is exactly as you found it. It never edits a theme file, and it only ever reads your theme to check whether it is switched on.

    read-only theme access

    No code and no theme editing

    No code, no theme editing, no Liquid. The setup wizard verifies each step for you and blocks the ones you are not ready for, with the reason written on the button. Every screen has a live preview, and every style change can be rolled back from a saved backup.

    Questions

    The things merchants ask first.

    Will it work with my theme?

    Varn works on any Online Store 2.0 theme, including Dawn and Horizon, because it is a theme app extension that drives your theme’s existing variant control rather than replacing it. It has specific handling for the way Dawn and Horizon re-render their pickers, and it detects your theme’s own native swatches and steps aside rather than rendering a second row.

    Will it slow my store down?

    The storefront script is deferred and split by feature, so a page downloads only the parts your settings actually use, and our build holds every one of them to a size budget. Swatch data arrives inside the page your theme already renders, so Varn adds no network request for it and shifts nothing on the page.

    What happens to my theme if I uninstall?

    Nothing is left behind. Varn never writes into a theme file and never injects a script tag, so removing the app removes the storefront code with it and your theme returns to its own dropdowns. Your setup stays saved on your Shopify products, so reinstalling brings it back, and Shopify cancels any paid plan the moment you uninstall.

    Does Varn change my products in Shopify?

    In one place, and only when you ask for it. When you save per-color photos, the first photo you picked for each color is set as that color’s variant image in Shopify, which is what makes the gallery follow the swatch in your theme. Varn only uses photos the product already has, and never uploads, edits or deletes a photo. Everything else Varn stores is kept separately rather than rewritten into your products.

    Can the wrong variant end up in a cart?

    No. Clicking a swatch selects the value in your theme’s own option control and fires its native change event. Your theme’s form is still what submits to Shopify, so there is no parallel cart path that could disagree with the picker. Varn also refuses to select a value your theme has disabled.

    Do I need to know any code?

    No. The setup guide on the home page checks your theme for you, and the product pages show only the options that are actually colors and fill in the colors they recognize. If you do want to go further, the Advance plan adds a custom CSS field, but nothing on any plan requires it.

    My color option isn’t called “Color”. Does that matter?

    Varn recognizes Color, Colour, Couleur, Farbe, Colore, Kleur and Farve automatically. If yours is called Shade, Finish or anything else, add the name once on the Appearance page and it is treated the same way everywhere.

    What is the AI actually doing?

    Two separate things, and we would rather be specific than mysterious about them. Auto-detect matches your option value names against a color dictionary spanning ten languages, applies only the matches it is confident about, and is free and unlimited on every plan. Set up with AI is for photos: it sends each product photo to a vision model, files it under the color it can see, and puts every color’s photos in the same order so switching color does not jump the shopper to a different angle. Shots that belong to no single color, such as a size chart, are kept visible for every color. That one is metered in AI usage credits, one credit per photo. Anything either is unsure about is left for you to set.

    Does it work in other languages?

    The storefront text is translated into 18 languages and follows whichever locale your shopper is browsing in. Assignments are translation-aware, so a color keeps its swatch across every translated version of the option. The admin interface itself is English.

    What do the paid plans actually add?

    Room, mostly. Starter is free forever and gives you unlimited color swatches on product pages, collections, search and quick view plus 250 AI usage credits a month, so plenty of stores never need to leave it. Paid plans raise three limits: how many products can have per-color photo galleries (5, 150, 1,500 or unlimited), how many AI usage credits you get each month (250, 1,500, 10,000 or 25,000) and how many product groups you can build (1, 5, 15 or 50). Grow and up also show swatches on products you have not set up yet, and Advance and Premium add swatch analytics and the advanced styling tools.

    What is an AI usage credit?

    One credit is one product photo the AI looks at. Credits are only spent by Set up with AI, which sends a photo to the vision model to find out which color it shows. Auto-detect, which reads your option value names, is free and unlimited on every plan. Each plan includes a monthly allowance, from 250 on Starter to 25,000 on Premium, and the app shows the date yours resets. If you run out, Set up with AI stops until the reset, while auto-detect and assigning photos by hand carry on as normal.

    How does the free trial work?

    Every paid plan is free for 7 days. Pick a plan, approve it on Shopify’s own confirmation screen, and the first 7 days cost nothing. Cancel inside those 7 days and you are never billed. Each store gets one trial, so switching plans during it keeps the days you have left rather than starting again, and trials run on the Starter allowance of 250 AI usage credits a month. Starter needs no trial at all, so you can equally start there and upgrade the day you outgrow it.
