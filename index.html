Variants on every page, in one click

    Varn turns your variant options into color swatches and image swatches on product pages, collections, search, and quick view. One-click setup names your whole catalog, and product grouping pulls separate products into one shared swatch row. Clicking a swatch selects the option in your theme’s own picker, so the wrong variant can never reach checkout.

    Product grouping

    One row, every color, and Varn builds it for you

    Plenty of stores sell every color as its own product. A shopper who lands on the Clay listing has no way of knowing other colors exist, unless they search. Our Shopify swatches app’s grouping feature fixes that: pick the products with multiple colors, and Varn shows them as one row of swatches on each product page.

    • Name the row: give it a caption shoppers will recognize, like Also in. It sits right above the swatches on every product in the group.
    • Set the order by dragging: add the products you want, then drag them into place. Whatever order you set is what shoppers see.
    • One group per product: a product can only belong to one group. If you try to add one that’s already in another, Varn flags it and alerts you to change it.
    • Or skip the manual work entirely: Varn can find the groups for you. It clusters titles that differ only by a color word and hands you a list to approve, edit, or dismiss.

    Every time a shopper clicks a color or adds one to their cart, Varn counts it, with no personal data collected and no cookies. Over time, our Shopify variants app builds a clear picture of which colors are actually working and which sold-out colors to restock.

    • Self-optimizing order: your best-selling colors can move to the front automatically, but never while a shopper’s cursor is on a swatch.

    Questions

    Frequently asked questions.

    What happens to my theme if I uninstall the Shopify variant app?

    Nothing is left behind. Varn never edits a theme file, so uninstalling returns your theme to its own dropdowns. Your product data stays untouched too.

    Can the wrong variant end up in a cart?

    No. Clicking a swatch selects the value in your theme’s own control, so your theme’s form is what submits to Shopify, not a separate system. Varn also will not select a value your theme has disabled.

    Will this Shopify color swatches app slow my store down?

    No, it will not. Varn is developed with lightweight code optimized for performance and will not slow your store down. It only loads the necessary features when needed, and swatch data comes with the page itself, so nothing extra has to load.

    Do I need to know any code to use Varn?

    No. The setup guide checks your theme, shows only real color options, and fills them in for you. A custom CSS field is there for anyone who wants to go further, but nothing requires it.

    Is your Shopify variant app really free forever?

    Yes. Starter is $0, with no time limit. It includes unlimited color swatches, unlimited auto-detect, agent readiness on every product, and swatches on collections, search, and quick view.

    How do I turn on Varn in my theme?

    On the Home page, click Activate on the first setup step. Turn the Varn switch on, then click Save. You do this once for each theme you use.

    What does Auto-detect do?

    It reads your option names, such as Navy blue or Forest green, and fills in the colors it is sure about. It is free on every plan, and you can change any of them afterwards. Photos are handled separately by Set up with AI on the Variant Images page.

    Does Varn work with my theme?

    Varn works with any Online Store 2.0 theme, which is almost every theme sold in the Shopify Theme Store today. Very old themes cannot run app embeds, so Varn will tell you if yours is one of them.

    What do the paid plans of your Shopify product options app add?

    Paid plans raise three limits: how many products you can set up with variant photos, how many product groups you can create, and your monthly AI usage credits. Advance and Premium also add advanced styling and swatch click analytics. Paying yearly saves 26%.

    What is an AI usage credit?

    One credit is one product photo the AI looks at, and it is only spent by Set up with AI. Auto-detect, which reads your option names, is free and unlimited on every plan. Each plan includes a monthly allowance, from 250 on Starter to 25,000 on Premium, and the app shows the date yours resets.

    How does the free trial work?

    Every paid plan starts with a 7 day free trial. Approve it on Shopify’s own confirmation screen, and cancel inside those 7 days to pay nothing. Each store gets one trial, so switching plans during it keeps the days you have left.

    My swatches are not showing on my store. What to do?

    Varn is usually not switched on for the theme you are looking at. Go to the Home page, click Activate on the first setup step, turn the switch on, and click Save.
