About this page

This page reads the health check the Varn dashboard runs against its own services. It refreshes about once a minute while the tab is open, and it reports what the check saw, including when the check itself cannot be reached.

A green day does not promise your store saw no error, and swatches keep working on your storefront even while the admin is degraded, because settings live on your own store as metafields. If something looks wrong on your store and this page says everything is fine, write to support@enstacked.com.
