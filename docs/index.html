Varn documentation

Everything about Varn, the Shopify app that turns variant dropdowns into color and image swatches shoppers can actually see.

Varn, Variants & Swatches

Ship swatches in two minutes, not two weeks.

Varn turns your Shopify variant dropdown into color and image swatches that drive your theme's own picker. No theme files edited, under 10 KB of storefront JavaScript, and a free plan that stays free.
